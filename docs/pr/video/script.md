# Video narration script (draft 1)

*For Karthik to review and record. Shot numbers match [storyboard.md](storyboard.md). Written 2026-11-13 by Claude (AI agent for Artem). Claims follow [../narrative.md](../narrative.md); the tagline is verbatim from [../STYLE.md](../STYLE.md).*

**Read time:** about 2:20 of speech at a calm 2.3 words a second; about 2:35 with the pauses and music beats. Per-scene times are in the table at the end.

## Recording notes

- Record each scene as its own take, with a second of room tone before and after. The video gets timed to your audio, so any pace that feels natural works. Pauses can run long, and the picture will wait for you.
- *(pause)* marks a beat for the picture. Quoted words are what a character types or sends, so read them a little lighter.
- Send WAV or M4A files, one per scene: `scene-0.wav` … `scene-6.wav`.

## Scene 0: Cold open

> There's no better way to connect than playing a game. *(0.1)*
>
> It's how families keep up, how strangers break the ice, and how old friends find their way back. *(0.2)*

## Scene 1: Old friends

> Maya and Jordan were best friends. Then life happened. *(1.1)*
>
> Five years later, "hey, long time" feels impossible to send. *(1.2)*
>
> But Jordan's score is right there in her Curios feed. *(1.3)*
>
> *(pause: she plays, 1.4)*
>
> A Curio asks so little of you. "Beat my 44?" needs no catch-up, no small talk and no right thing to say. *(1.5)*
>
> Jordan gets 51. *(1.6)* Then: "rematch?" And they're talking again. *(1.7)*

## Scene 2: Across generations

> Rosa is 74. Her grandson Leo is 19, and three time zones away. *(2.1)*
>
> She sends him a Curio: "I played this and thought of you." *(2.2)*
>
> Same Curio, same seed, each in their own time. *(2.3)*
>
> Their family Group lines up the results. Grandma's won four in a row. *(2.4)*

## Scene 3: Meeting someone new

> Priya just moved to a new city and joined a climbing Group. She doesn't know anyone yet. *(3.1)*
>
> But on the Group's weekly board, Dev is one spot above her. *(3.2)*
>
> Add friend. Best of 5. *(3.3)*
>
> Same Curios, same seeds, so it's fair. No small talk required. *(3.4)*
>
> By Saturday, they're climbing together. *(3.5)*

## Scene 4: A big Group

> A whole Group can play together. Harry Potter fans add their own questions, and Muse builds one quiz from all of them, every name credited. *(4.1)*
>
> Put a sound behind it. Race the weekly board. *(4.2)* Remix it, with credit. *(4.3)*
>
> And brands and creators get a Curio of their own. *(4.4)*

## Scene 5: Limitless creativity

> And anyone can make one. Just say the idea. *(5.1)*
>
> A story quiz from today's photos. *(5.2)* Mr. Beast-style brainrot trivia. *(5.3)* A golden retriever's first day as a waiter. *(5.4)* Dodge the fog over Twin Peaks. *(5.5)*
>
> Muse Spark reads it. Muse Wonder plans it. Muse Image paints it. And five bots play every game before you do. *(5.6)*
>
> Other apps are feature-first. Curios are fun-first: the connection comes with the play. *(5.7)*

## Scene 6: Close

> Muse Curios. A new feature in Facebook. *(6.1)*
>
> *(pause)*
>
> When the world is your muse, how will you create the content of tomorrow? *(6.2)*
