The AI stack

Four Muse models, one harness that makes them reliable.

01 · SPARK
Understands the request
Then retrieval over 1,280 game types: the right one is in the top 5 for 94% of 523 test requests.
02 · EMBER
Writes the brief
Mood, energy, competitiveness and audience, in about 2 s.
03 · WONDER
Writes and builds the game
The spec, the rules and the content, then up to 3 repair rounds.
04 · IMAGE
Paints the art
Characters and scenes, drawn for this game.

Then it has to pass

A 5-bot playtest gate and a score check. About 75% of games pass; the rest are repaired or never shown.

Server, not phone

Muse, the data, ranking and score checks all run on our server. The app only shows the game and plays it. The Muse key never leaves the server.

Harness numbers: docs/pr/09-evidence.md and the harness evals on main.

Shared rituals bring people back

A daily game with your Group, a rematch with a friend, a best of 5 with someone new: a reason to log on again and again, on your schedule.
Creators get something to show; viewers get something to give back: a score, a remix, a challenge.
We pitch coming back, not traffic to the rest of the app: the research shows habit and retention, not spillover.
